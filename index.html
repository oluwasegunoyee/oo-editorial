About Me

Helping Authors Publish with Confidence

Hi, I'm Oluwasegun Oyeleye

I'm a professional Book Editor, Book Formatter, Beta Reader, and Publishing Consultant dedicated to helping authors transform manuscripts into polished, publication-ready books.

Writing a book is a major achievement, but preparing it for publication requires careful editing, professional formatting, and expert guidance. I work closely with self-published and independent authors to ensure every manuscript is clear, engaging, and professionally presented.

Whether you're publishing your first book or adding another title to your catalogue, my goal is to provide personalized support, honest feedback, and high-quality results.
